## Overview

This is the site you're on. It's my portfolio, and the third version of it. I think of it as a playground more than a résumé.

It has three sides, and you switch between them in the header. The Front is the pages. The Back is a terminal over the same content, where you `ls`, `cd` and `cat` your way through. The IA is a chat that answers about me and the site.

Everything exists in English and in Portuguese, at `/pt`.

## What's in it

- A home that is a door to each page
- `/person`, with photos, a playlist, my Apple Watch rings, the weather in Pernambuco, stickers and a shelf of what I've finished
- `/professional`, with my career, my stack, the apps I work in and my GitHub contributions
- Notes and projects, in MDX, each one with its Portuguese twin
- A wall of love, with letters from people I worked with, by invitation
- A contact page that is a postcard, and sends an email
- A memory game in the footer, where the pairs are the pages, and a board for the fastest rounds
- Abimaela, my cat, in the corner, reacting to what you do

## Decisions

- **entrepta is copied in, not installed.** The site has no runtime dependency on it. A change in entrepta only arrives here when I bring it in on purpose.
- **The chat has a model behind it, and the model is the last guard, not the only one.** A question is screened before it reaches it, and the answer is screened too, so it isn't streamed. The limits live in Postgres. Without the key, the chat answers from a script. The cap that holds even if my code is wrong is the credit limit on the key itself.
- **Pages that read Postgres aren't cached, on purpose.** An entry I published ten minutes ago should be there, and a ring shouldn't be frozen at this morning's numbers. It costs a couple of indexed queries.
- **`/admin` is behind WorkOS AuthKit and an email allowlist.** Anyone else gets a 404, never a 403, because a 403 confirms the page exists.
- **On the memory board, the server keeps the time, not the browser.** A name only shows up after I approve it.

## Stack

- Next.js (App Router), TypeScript and Tailwind
- entrepta, my design system
- MDX with Velite, in two languages with next-intl
- Postgres on Supabase, with Drizzle
- Hono for the API
- WorkOS AuthKit for the admin
- Resend for email
- OpenRouter for the chat
