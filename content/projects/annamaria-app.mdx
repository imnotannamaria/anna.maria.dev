## Overview

This is my personal website. I use it to publish projects and blog posts.

It also includes a contact page with email sending, plus static pages like About and Uses.

## Main sections

- Home with a short intro and quick links
- Projects with MDX case studies
- Blog with MDX posts
- Contact with a form that sends email

## Stack

- Next.js (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- MDX with Velite
- Resend for sending email
